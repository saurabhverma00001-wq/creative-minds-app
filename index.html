About the Director

Saurabh Verma is the visionary Founder and Director of The Creative Minds Coaching Institute, located in Rajajipuram, Lucknow.

An alumnus of the prestigious City Montessori School (CMS) and an ICSE board background, he holds a professional degree in Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Shri Ram Swaroop Memorial University.

With over 10+ years of rich teaching experience, he specializes in simplifying complex concepts in Mathematics, Science, and Programming, steering students toward absolute academic excellence.
