🏆 About Creative Minds

Lead Instructor & Founder

Established Since 2016 | Rajajipuram, Lucknow

Creative Minds has been a premier institution in Rajajipuram, Lucknow since 2016, dedicated to shaping bright futures through exceptional academic mentorship and advanced technical skill-building.

We offer comprehensive coaching in core school subjects including Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), History & Civics (H.C.G.), and English for ICSE and CBSE board students.

In addition to academics, we empower students with cutting-edge professional technical courses such as App Making, Web Development, Video Editing, Graphics Design, and Creative 3D Model Crafting to prepare them for tomorrow's technology landscape.
