🏆 About Creative Minds

Saurabh Verma

B.Tech (ECE) | CMS Lucknow Alumnus

Founder & Lead Instructor (Teaching Since 2016)

Creative Minds was established in 2016 in Rajajipuram, Lucknow by Saurabh Verma (CMS Lucknow alumnus and B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering). With over 10 years of rich teaching experience, we specialize in mentoring students, especially from City Montessori School (CMS).

We offer comprehensive academic coaching in Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Computer Science, English, History, Civics, and Geography for ICSE and CBSE boards.

As a tech innovator, Saurabh Verma also trains students in modern programming and tech skills including Java, Python, C++, App Making, Web Development, Video Editing, and Graphics.
